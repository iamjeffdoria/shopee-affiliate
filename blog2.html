Smartwatch Review: Stay Fit and Connected

Published on February 24, 2026 | By Aika Romerro

Smartwatches have evolved from simple time-tellers to powerful fitness companions and communication hubs. In this comprehensive review, we'll explore the key features that make a smartwatch essential for modern living, and why the one from Shopee stands out.

Fitness Tracking Capabilities

Modern smartwatches track steps, heart rate, calories burned, and even sleep patterns. Advanced models include ECG monitoring and blood oxygen levels. GPS functionality allows for accurate distance and pace monitoring during runs, hikes, or cycling. Many also offer guided workouts and personalized fitness goals.

Pros: Comprehensive health metrics, motivational features.

Cons: Accuracy can vary, requires consistent wearing.

Notifications and Connectivity

Stay connected without constantly checking your phone. Receive calls, texts, and app notifications directly on your wrist. Smartwatches sync with smartphones to display weather, calendar events, and music controls. Customizable watch faces and apps extend functionality, from payment options to voice assistants.

Pros: Convenience, reduces phone dependency.

Cons: Can be distracting if not managed properly.

Battery Life and Design

Look for watches with at least 24-48 hours of battery life between charges. Water resistance (IP68 or 5ATM rating) is crucial for active lifestyles. Comfortable bands made from silicone or metal, and intuitive touchscreens or rotating bezels enhance usability. Screen size typically ranges from 1.2 to 1.8 inches for optimal readability.

Pros: Durable, stylish, long-lasting battery.

Cons: Heavier than traditional watches.
